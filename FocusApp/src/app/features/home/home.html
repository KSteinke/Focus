Stay focus and do more!

Boost your focus and well-being with my free productivity app! Featuring a customizable Pomodoro timer, relaxing soundscapes like ocean waves or café ambience, and dynamic backgrounds to set the perfect mood. Stay productive, stay inspired — all without paying a dime.

Use the app for FREE!

Features

Pomodoro Timer

Stay in control of your productivity with a Pomodoro timer. Set your own work and break intervals, whether you prefer the traditional 25/5 cycle, extended focus sessions, or shorter sprints. The timer helps you maintain focus while avoiding burnout.

Mix & Match Sounds

Explore a wide variety of high-quality ambient sounds, including the calming rhythm of ocean waves, the soothing hum of white noise, the lively chatter of a coffee shop and more. Go beyond a single track by blending multiple sounds together – layer ocean waves with gentle rain, or mix café ambience with bird sounds to craft your own personalized atmosphere. 

Dynamic Backgrounds

Enhance your focus not only with sound but also with visuals. Switch between a variety of backgrounds, from calming natural landscapes to minimalistic gradient themes. The visuals adapt to your mood and add a refreshing touch to every session, making your workspace both functional and inspiring.

About Me

Hi, I’m Kacper! Like many people, I often struggle with staying focused. Over time, I discovered that listening to certain sounds — especially white noise — makes a huge difference for my concentration. On top of that, the Pomodoro technique became another tool I rely on to stay productive.

That’s why I decided to combine these two methods into one simple app. What started as something I built for myself quickly turned into an idea I wanted to share with others. My goal is to help people find focus, calm, and motivation in a way that feels natural and enjoyable. And so, this project was born — created with passion, and shared with joy.
